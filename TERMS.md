# User Terms

Last updated: 21 September 2026.

The service is intended for users aged 18 and over. By pressing the recognition button, a user confirms that they are at least 18 and permits the submitted photo and/or meal description to be sent to Google Gemini for analysis.

FatSecret supplies product, portion and nutrition data and receives diary entries only after a separate final confirmation. By using content supplied through FatSecret, users agree to the [fatsecret Platform API Terms of Use](https://platform.fatsecret.com/terms).

Recognition, product selection, estimated mass and calculated nutrition values may be inaccurate. Users must review products, portions and calories/macronutrients before saving and should refer to packaging for authoritative product information. The bot does not provide medical, dietary or diagnostic advice and is not a substitute for professional consultation.

Use of the bot is also subject to the applicable terms of Telegram, Google Gemini and FatSecret. The current data handling is described in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
